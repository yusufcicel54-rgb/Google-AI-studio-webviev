# Google-AI-studio-webviev
# ⚡ Google AI Studio Mobile (Unofficial)

A blazing-fast, buttery-smooth Android client for **Google AI Studio**, built with Mozilla's **GeckoView** engine.

Tired of shitty, laggy WebView wrappers on the Play Store that break your Google Login with `disallowed_useragent` errors? This app bundles a full standalone Firefox rendering engine to provide a native-like, zero-compromise experience.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Silky Smooth Performance:** Renders AI Studio like butter. No random frame drops or WebView stutter.
- **Hassle-Free Google Auth:** Powered by GeckoView (independent Firefox engine), so Google doesn't bitch about "disallowed user-agents". Full Google Sign-in works out of the box.
- **Modern Web APIs:** Hardware acceleration, desktop/mobile responsive UI handling, and isolated storage.
- **Zero Ads, Zero Trackers:** Just the pure AI Studio experience.

---

## 📦 Why is the APK ~90MB?

Unlike generic Android WebView apps that rely on your system's Chromium (which Google easily blocks and lags like shit), this app bundles the **entire GeckoView runtime**. 

You are basically carrying a dedicated, optimized browser engine tuned specifically to run AI Studio with 100% compatibility. Quality requires megabytes, baby.

*(Note: We provide both Universal and architecture-specific `arm64-v8a` builds in Releases).*

---

## 📥 Download & Install

1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases) tab.
2. Download the latest `.apk` (Choose `arm64-v8a` for modern devices, or `universal` if you're not sure).
3. Install on your Android phone and log in with your Google account.
4. Enjoy testing models on the go.
